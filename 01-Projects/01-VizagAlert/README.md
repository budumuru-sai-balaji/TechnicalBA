
# VizagAlert – Civic Infrastructure Alert System

## Project Overview

VizagAlert is a self-directed Technical Business Analysis case study focused on designing a real-time, multi-department civic complaint management and routing platform for Visakhapatnam.

The proposed platform aims to replace fragmented, unaccountable complaint reporting through a structured workflow involving complaint submission, automated department routing, SLA monitoring, field-level resolution and citizen feedback.

## Business Scope

The proposed solution covers six departments:

- Fire
- GVMC
- Water
- Electricity
- Traffic
- Health

## Important Project Disclaimer

This is an independently developed Business Analysis case study and prototype.

Business requirements, workflows, SLA targets and technical specifications represent proposed designs and assumptions. They have not been validated or approved by the relevant municipal authorities.

Prototype functionality should be distinguished from production implementation.
